# Original sample assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool; twelve cropped WebP assets are saved here and have no dependency on `your-store`. These are illustrative inventory examples, not images of a merchant's real products.

Prompt: A precisely aligned 4-column by 3-row photorealistic lighting catalog contact sheet, twelve equal rectangular panels with no gutters, borders, text, logos or watermarks. Each adjacent pair shows the same product, camera angle and interior in natural daylight with the fixture off, then at night with the fixture on and realistic warm illumination. Row one: ivory linen dome pendant in a beige plaster room with travertine plinth; ivory ceramic mushroom table lamp on oak. Row two: tall thin black floor lamp with linen shade beside a lounge chair; matte black cylindrical track spotlight against an angled plaster wall. Row three: white recessed downlight in a plaster ceiling corner; brass ring ceiling lamp in a minimal living room. Editorial architectural photography with soft textures and neutral beige, white, black and brass. Lamps centered with space around their silhouette for catalog cropping. Night panels are truly darker rooms with glowing practical fixtures, not color inversions.

Files: `pendant-off/on.webp`, `table-off/on.webp`, `floor-off/on.webp`, `spotlight-off/on.webp`, `downlight-off/on.webp`, `ceiling-off/on.webp`. The original generated sheet was 1536×1024 pixels; the card assets are approximately 384×341. Replace them with your own higher-resolution renders for a production catalog. Aim for at least 1200 pixels on the long edge and identical framing for the two product views.
